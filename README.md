# Case Summary

Five test cases are considered, covering different geometries and fluid rheologies.

| Case | Geometry | Fluid |
|------|----------|-------|
| [Case 1](#case-1) | Rectangular channel with an additional wall segment on the left boundary | Simple material (low complexity rheology) |
| [Case 2](#case-2) | Rectangular channel with an additional wall segment on the left boundary | Hard material (high complexity rheology) |
| [Case 3](#case-3) | Plain rectangular channel (no additional wall) | Simple material (low complexity rheology) |
| [Case 4](#case-4) | Rectangular channel with a cylindrical obstacle in the middle | Simple material (low complexity rheology) |
| [Case 5](#case-5) | Channel with varying height, constricted at the middle section | Simple material (low complexity rheology) |
